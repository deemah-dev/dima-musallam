Background & Learning Path

My Journey

✦ Solid Programming Foundations

I built my technical foundations through ProgrammingAdvices and Dr. Mohammed Abu-Hadhoud's structured roadmap. This stage gave me a strong understanding of programming fundamentals, problem-solving, algorithms, data structures, object-oriented programming, and core software development concepts. More importantly, it shaped how I approach problems: breaking them down, understanding their underlying logic, and designing solutions rather than simply writing code.

✦ Web Development & ASP.NET Core

I then focused on web development and backend engineering through the Metigator platform by Eng. Issam Abdilnabi. Throughout this stage, I practiced software engineering concepts such as error handling, validation, documentation, caching, observability, testing, and clean architecture. I applied these practices while building Mechanic Shop, a real-world-style application that brought together the concepts I had learned and challenged me to think beyond individual features toward the design and maintainability of an entire application.
